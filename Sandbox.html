Sandbox

Sandbox games are games that offer freedom to explore, build, experiment, and create your own goals with minimal restrictions. These games are often shown from top down, but some are first person because of the difference in genre.

Players can choose their own activities, build structures, collect resources, or complete missions at their own pace. Sandbox games often features open worlds and encourage creativity and experimentation.

Here is the list of sandbox games:

Minecraft

PC, Nintendo Switch, Wii U, PS and Xbox

Minecraft is an amazing sandbox survival game where you can express your creativity. With the simple but beautiful graphics, the game offer endless possibilities for exploration or building.

You can build gorgeous builds, explore a randomly generated world and even fight a few bosses. Whether you prefer to play alone or with friends, Minecraft always has something fun to offer.

It’s a great game because of its freedom and possibilities for creativity. Every world and playthrough is a unique experience, which is why Minecraft is still so popular and fun after all this time. I recommend anyone that has never played Minecraft to try the game out.

Minecraft was almost called something else. Before Minecraft it was called Cave Game during early development.

Cities: Skylines

PC, PS and Xbox

Cities: Skylines is a great city builder and sandbox. You have a lot of freedom to create a city that matches your vision, from small towns to huge metropolises.

You can build roads, public transport, and services to keep your citizens happy. The game offers a lot of depth and creativity, making every city unique. Although managing traffic and trying to not go broke can be challenging, building your city and watching it being built is extremely satisfying.

Cities: Skylines is a great game for anyone who enjoys creativity and city builders.

Sandbox games are called sandbox because they offer creativity and tools just like in a real sandbox.

Raft

PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

Raft is an amazing survival sandbox game where you have to survive in the middle of the ocean with a raft that you expand through your playthrough. You collect resources, craft tools, and expand your raft while searching for islands and progressing through the story.

The game offers a perfect mix of survival, exploration, and creativity. Playing with friends improves the experience in my opinion, as you work together to build a bigger raft and progress the story.

I recommend this game to anyone who wants to have a relaxing time building and collecting.
